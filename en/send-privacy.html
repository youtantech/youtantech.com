Xiyne Gallery Send privacy policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Below is the privacy policy of Xiyne Gallery Send, the transfer companion of Xiyne Gallery, as shown inside the app.

Xiyne Gallery Send ("the app") is the transfer companion of Xiyne Gallery, developed and operated by Kunming Youtan Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information about you and what rights you have. Please read it carefully before using the app.

1. In short

The app only moves what you choose in Xiyne Gallery directly between two phones, through no server and over no mobile data. We do not collect, store or upload your photos, files or any personal information. The app has no accounts and contains no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking or push tools.

2. How each feature uses your information

1) Face-to-face sending: when you choose photos, videos or files in Xiyne Gallery, the gallery lets the app read those files; the permission to read them is taken back as soon as the transfer ends, the page is closed or a time limit passes. The app cannot reach anything else in your gallery.

2) Receiving: photos and videos received are saved to the phone's gallery, other files to the "Download/Xiyne" folder. Files that did not arrive in full are deleted, so no broken files are left.

3) Phone-to-phone move: moves the photos and videos, categories and favourites, people's names, app settings and the private album of Xiyne Gallery from the old phone to the new one. The private album travels as an encrypted backup and is never decrypted on the way; the app cannot read it.

4) Sending to an iPhone: once the other phone has joined the temporary network this phone sets up, it downloads the files you chose from this phone in its browser. The download address carries a random key, is valid only during this transfer, and the network joins only the two phones.

5) The app keeps no record of transfers and no copies of your photos or files.

3. Permissions the app uses

Each permission is asked for only when you use the feature that needs it, with its purpose shown at the same time. You can refuse it, or turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings › Apps › Permissions; the feature that needs it then stops working, and nothing else is affected.

1) Camera (CAMERA): to scan the QR code on the other phone. The camera is on only on the scanning page; nothing it sees is saved or uploaded.

2) Nearby devices (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, Android 13 and later) or location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, Android 12 and earlier): the system requires this of apps that set up a Wi-Fi Direct connection or a local hotspot between two phones. The app never reads or uses your location.

3) Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the progress in the notification bar while a transfer goes on in the background.

4) Granted by the system without asking you: network, viewing and changing the Wi-Fi state (to connect the two phones directly and transfer; no server on the internet is contacted), foreground service and keeping the phone awake (so that a transfer goes on when you leave the page or lock the screen).

4. Third-party SDKs and sharing

The app uses no third-party SDK. QR codes are made and read with the open-source ZXing library, which runs only on your phone and collects and sends nothing. We do not share, transfer or publicly disclose your personal information.

5. Security

The app accepts requests only from Xiyne Gallery, checked by its signature, and can read only the files handed to it for the transfer at hand. Transfers run only on the local network between the two phones, never over the internet.

6. Your rights

The app keeps no personal information, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete; the photos and files you receive can be seen and deleted at any time in the phone's gallery and file manager. You can turn off permissions in your phone's settings, or tap "Withdraw consent" at the bottom of the "Privacy policy" page on the app's home page, after which the app closes and asks for your consent again before it can be used. The app has no accounts, so there is nothing to close.

7. Children

The app is meant mainly for adults. Children under 14 should use it only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with the law or with the app. The new version comes with an app update and is shown in the app; for material changes the app tells you and asks for your consent again.
