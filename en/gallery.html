Xiyne Gallery

One sentence brings the moment back. Private photos stay yours. Fully offline.

Xiyne Gallery is a photo gallery for Android that understands what is in your pictures, so you can find them in your own words, and a vault that keeps the private ones locked away. Everything happens on your phone: the app asks for no internet access, has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What it does

  • Search by description: type "sunset at the beach" or "dog on the grass" and the photos appear. 18 languages; also by date, place, person, text in the picture and file name.
  • Search by image, similar photos, box search: draw a box around part of a photo to find photos that contain it.
  • Private album: AES-256 encryption file by file, password or fingerprint, decoy password, hidden entry, encrypted backup and encrypted sharing.
  • Organising: twenty-odd automatic categories, people, places, favourites, your own categories.
  • Clean-up: duplicates, similar shots, blurred and closed-eye photos; a 30-day recycle bin.
  • Editing: crop, straighten, adjust, 22 filters, mosaic, beauty, watermark; the original is never changed.
  • Safe sharing: guest viewing (hand your phone over and only the chosen photos can be seen), clean sharing (location and device data removed).
  • More formats: AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, PSD, SVG, CDR and more; JPEG XL through "Open file".
  • Fast: opens in about 0.3 s; indexing on the NPU or GPU where the chip allows.

Privacy

Photos, the index and passwords stay on your phone and are never uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Get it

Coming to Google Play. Android 8.0 and later; NPU acceleration on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and similar chips.
